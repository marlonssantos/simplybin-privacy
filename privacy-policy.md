# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-29

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **SimplyBin: Inventory Tracker** ("SimplyBin™", package `com.simplybin.myapp`), developed and operated by **Nolram Labs, LLC** ("we", "us", "our"). It explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

**Contact:** nolramlabs@gmail.com

## Summary

- Your boxes, items, notes, and photos are stored **only on your device**. We never receive them.
- Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. We receive only the basic account details those services share.
- We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

SimplyBin™ stores the following in the app's private storage on your device:

- Storage boxes, items, descriptions, quantities, categories, locations, and QR codes you create.
- Photos you take or choose for boxes and items.
- App settings and preferences, and reminders you schedule.

This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. It is removed when you uninstall the app or choose to delete it in the app. Data you export (for example CSV, PDF, or backup files) or share is saved or sent wherever you choose, and is then governed by that destination.

## Information we collect when you sign in

Creating an account is optional. If you sign in with **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**, we use Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) to create an account record containing:

- A unique account ID.
- Your name and email address, as shared by Apple or Google (Apple may provide a private relay address).
- Your sign-in provider.

We use this information only to identify your account and to link your Premium purchase to it.

## Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to validate and manage purchases and subscription status. RevenueCat receives your account ID (used as your app user ID), purchase and subscription information, and basic device and app information needed to provide this service.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to scan QR codes and take photos of boxes and items. Images are processed on your device. Item suggestions from photos use on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit); images are not sent to us.
- **Photos and files:** to pick images and to import or export data files you select.
- **Notifications:** to show reminders that you schedule. They are generated locally on your device.
- **Internet:** to sign in, check Premium status, and check for app updates.

## How we use information

We use information only to provide and maintain the app, to sign you in, to provide Premium features, to respond to support requests, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the app, who process it on our behalf:

- **Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In)**
- **Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store)**
- **Google Play** (purchases)
- **RevenueCat** (purchase management)

We may also disclose information if required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This deletes your sign-in record and its link to your Apple or Google account. You can also email us at the address above from the email linked to your account, and we will delete it within 30 days. Boxes, items, and photos are stored only on your device and are kept unless you choose "Also delete all boxes, items, and photos from this device" or uninstall the app. App stores and payment processors may retain transaction records as required by law.

## Security

Information we handle is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because your inventory data lives on your device, you are responsible for protecting your device and any backups or exports you create.

## Children's privacy

SimplyBin™ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.
